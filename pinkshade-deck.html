The Problem

02 · FORMATSOptions being explored

Three Ways to Try It

We are exploring three formats for the same idea: a warm, vibrant tint on everyday glasses. Each one trades off simplicity, cost, and how long it lasts.

ADHESIVE FILM

A thin optical film cut to fit and pressed onto the lens. Simplest and lowest cost.

TESTING FIRST

CLIP-ON

A tinted attachment that clips onto existing frames. Reusable across frames, but more parts to fit and handle.

CUSTOM LENS TINT

A tint built into the lens with a manufacturing partner. Most durable, longest timeline, a later-stage option.
